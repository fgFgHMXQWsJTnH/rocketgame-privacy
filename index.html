Privacy Policy for Rocket Slingshot

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Rocket Slingshot ("the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game application.

Information Collection and Use

Rocket Slingshot does not directly collect any personal information from users. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data including:

  • Advertising ID (a unique, user-resettable identifier for advertising)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (interactions with ads)
  • IP address

This data is used to serve personalized advertisements and measure ad performance. For more information about how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Types of Ads

The App displays only optional rewarded video ads. Users can choose to watch these ads to unlock game stages early. No ads are forced or displayed without user consent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties except as required to provide advertising services through Google AdMob.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information from users of any age. All game progress is stored locally on the user's device and is never transmitted to any servers. If you have privacy concerns, please uninstall the App.

Data Retention

Game progress data (levels unlocked, stars earned) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any servers. This data remains on your device until you uninstall the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. Google AdMob will present you with consent options when you first use the App. You can withdraw consent at any time through your device settings.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to request information about the categories of personal information collected and how it is shared. Since the App uses Google AdMob, please refer to Google's privacy policies for detailed information about data practices.

How to Manage Your Consent

You can change your ad personalization preferences at any time:

  • In your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID

For more information about your privacy rights and choices, visit: https://www.google.com/settings/ads

To withdraw consent, uninstall and reinstall the app, or use the device settings above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

Email: rocketslingshotdev [at] gmail [dot]com

Note: This app does not collect or store any personal information on remote servers. All game data is stored locally on your device.
