Privacy Policy for Rocket Slingshot

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Rocket Slingshot is an offline mobile game. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

The app does not collect, use, or share personal information. It does not connect to the internet and requests no Android permissions. No account, sign-in, or profile is required to play.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress, scores, and settings are saved in the app's local files on your device. They remain on the device and are not accessible to the developer.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no information from users of any age, including children.

Data Deletion

Your game data can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data in the device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

This page is the current version of the policy. It is updated when the app's privacy practices change, and the date at the top of the page shows the version in force.
